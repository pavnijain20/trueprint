# GlowLeaf Brand Kit
**Purpose:** Demo/testing only  
**Status:** Synthetic demo data; not verified real product information  
**Canonical product name:** GlowLeaf Serum

## 1. Tone Rules
1. Use a friendly, clear, and helpful tone.
2. Keep sentences simple and easy to understand.
3. Avoid ALL CAPS, excessive exclamation marks, and pressure-based language.
4. Do not make medical, guaranteed, or unsupported product claims.
5. Always spell the product name exactly as “GlowLeaf Serum”.

## 2. Banned Terms / Claims
Do not use these unless the team has valid supporting evidence and approval:
- “miracle”
- “cure”
- “100% guaranteed”
- “clinically proven”
- “removes acne”
- “works for every skin type”
- “doctors recommend”

## 3. Preferred Terms
Use neutral, non-guaranteeing wording:
- “learn more about GlowLeaf Serum”
- “explore our product information”
- “add it to your skincare routine”
- “share your feedback”
- “supports your skincare routine”
